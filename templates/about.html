About this project

This UPI Fraud Detection project was developed by 4 students of 3rd year,under the guidance of our faculty mentor. It demonstrates how machine learning and rule-based checks can help identify potentially fraudulent transactions in real time.

The app provides a simple interface to upload CSV data, predict the safety of transactions, and review basic activity via a dashboard. It is designed as an academic project to explore practical applications of data-driven decision-making in digital payments.
